Formula help
  • Use + − * / ( ) like a calculator, e.g. PTS + REB + AST
  • IF(MIN > 20, PTS / MIN, 0) picks one result or another
  • ROUND(x, 2), AVERAGE(a, b, c), SUM(a, b), sqrt(x), max(a, b), min(a, b), abs(x)
  • Percentages like FG_PCT are on a 0–100 scale (46.4 means 46.4%)
  • You can use your own saved stats inside new formulas
  • Names can't have spaces. Use letters, numbers and underscores.
